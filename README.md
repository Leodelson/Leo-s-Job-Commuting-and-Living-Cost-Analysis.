# Leo's Job Commuting and Living Cost Insights:
## Problem Statement:
Leo is trying to determine the most cost-efficient and balanced living option by comparing commute distances, transportation expenses, and overall living costs. Without a clear breakdown of these factors, it becomes difficult for him to decide which location best supports both financial stability and a healthy work-life balance. This analysis helps Leo make an informed decision by evaluating total commuting cost, living expenses, and daily convenience across his available housing options.

## Summary of Key Insights:
Commuting Costs: Lugbe Living options have lower daily, monthly and annual commuting costs compared to Gwagwalada.
Annual Cost Comparison by Living Option: Gwagwalada have lower house rent compared to Lugbe. 
Work-Life Balance: Lugbe Living options with shorter commutes generally provide a better work-life balance compared to Gwagwalada.

## Recommendations:
Commuting Costs: Leo should consider living in Lugbe or closer areas to reduce commuting expenses.
Annual Cost Comparison: Choose a living option that balances rent and transport costs, such as Lugbe.
Work-Life Balance: Prioritize living in Lugbe for shorter commute times to enhance work-life balance.
## Conclusion:
This analysis helps Leo understand the commuting and living costs for different living options, aiding in choosing a cost-efficient option while maintaining work-life balance.
